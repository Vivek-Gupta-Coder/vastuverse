The idea

What is Vastu Shastra?

Vastu Shastra is a traditional Indian body of knowledge concerned with the planning and arrangement of buildings and spaces. It draws on ideas about orientation, proportion, spatial use and the relationship between people and their surroundings.

How it works

Orientation, made easier to explore.

On supported mobile devices, the browser may provide orientation events. Sensor access can require HTTPS, permission, and a compatible browser. Desktop computers often do not have a compass sensor.

  • Press “Enable device compass” and approve the browser permission if prompted.
  • If sensor readings are unavailable, use the manual selector to rotate the compass illustration.
  • For reliable building measurements, use a properly calibrated physical or digital compass and confirm the result on site.

Tradition and tools

Where modern technology can help.

Digital tools can make traditional concepts easier to study, document and discuss. They do not automatically validate traditional claims.

⌖

Digital orientation

Device sensors and compass tools can help users inspect direction, subject to calibration and hardware limitations.

▦

Interactive floor plans

A simple grid makes it easy to experiment with room arrangements and compare alternative layouts.

⌕

Searchable knowledge

A structured JSON database lets learners search key terms and browse concise, topic-specific explanations.

Educational note: Vastu recommendations vary across texts, regions and practitioners. Treat them as cultural and historical guidance; evaluate claims about health, luck or outcomes critically. Building safety, accessibility, structural engineering and local regulations take priority.
